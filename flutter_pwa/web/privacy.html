隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-22

本政策說明 Prompt Atelier 公開版如何處理瀏覽器資料、第三方服務與廣告。

This policy explains how the public Prompt Atelier handles browser data, third-party services, and advertising.

本機資料 / Local data

提示詞、介面偏好與工具設定通常只儲存在目前瀏覽器的 localStorage，本站沒有自有後端接收這些提示詞。匯出與匯入由瀏覽器在本機處理；使用公開角色查詢或外部連結時，瀏覽器才會直接連線至對應第三方。清除網站資料會移除本機記憶。

Prompts, interface preferences, and tool settings are normally stored only in this browser's localStorage; the site has no first-party backend that receives those prompts. Export and import are processed locally by the browser. Public character lookup and external links connect the browser directly to the relevant third party. Clearing site data removes local memory.

Google AdSense 與廣告 / Google AdSense and advertising

本站使用 Google AdSense。Google 與其他第三方廣告供應商可能因廣告投放、個人化、成效衡量、防止濫用或服務安全而在瀏覽器中放置或讀取 Cookie，並使用網路信標、IP 位址或其他識別碼。廣告是否個人化會依所在地、使用者同意、Google 設定及適用規範而異。

This site uses Google AdSense. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may place or read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers for ad delivery, personalization, measurement, abuse prevention, or service security. Whether ads are personalized depends on location, consent, Google settings, and applicable requirements.

同意與選擇 / Consent and choices

在依法需要同意的地區，本站會配合 AdSense 中設定的 Google 或其他經認證同意管理平台（CMP）取得廣告相關選擇。你也可以透過上述 Google 廣告設定管理個人化廣告，或在瀏覽器中清除 Cookie 與網站資料。

Where consent is legally required, the site relies on Google's CMP or another certified consent management platform configured in AdSense to collect advertising choices. You can also manage personalized ads through Google Ads Settings above or clear cookies and site data in your browser.

第三方服務 / Third-party services

公開角色查詢、外部連結及廣告服務可能連線至 Google、Jikan、MyAnimeList 或其各自的服務供應商。這些第三方依其自身政策處理資料，本站無法控制其系統。

Public character lookup, external links, and advertising may connect to Google, Jikan, MyAnimeList, or their respective service providers. Those third parties process data under their own policies, which this site does not control.

政策更新與聯絡 / Updates and contact

功能、廣告或法規要求變更時，本政策可能更新。問題可透過專案的 GitHub Issues 提出。

This policy may be updated when features, advertising, or regulatory requirements change. Questions may be submitted through the project's GitHub Issues.
